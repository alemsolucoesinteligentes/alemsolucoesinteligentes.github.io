Privacy Policy

How we handle user data in our applications and games

Summary: Alem Soluções Inteligentes is committed to protecting the privacy of all users of our applications and games. This policy clearly describes what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your personal information.

1. Introduction

Alem Soluções Inteligentes ("we", "our" or "company") develops mobile applications, games and interactive simulations. This Privacy Policy applies to all our products available on platforms such as Google Play Store, Apple App Store and web applications.

By using our applications and games, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. We recommend that you read this policy carefully before using our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect different types of information depending on the specific application you use:

2.1 Information Provided Directly

  • Account Data: Username, email address (when applicable)
  • Profile Settings: Game preferences, notification settings
  • User-Generated Content: Scores, achievements, game progress

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Data: Device model, operating system, application version
  • Usage Data: Time spent in the application, resources used, interactions
  • Technical Data: IP address, device identifiers, crash and diagnostic data
  • Location Data: Approximate location (only when necessary for specific functionalities and with your consent)

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Services: If you choose to log in using third-party services (Google, Facebook, Apple), we may receive basic profile information
  • Analytics: We use analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase) to understand application usage

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide and Improve Services: Operate our applications, personalize your experience, develop new features
  • Communication: Send updates, notifications about in-game events, respond to your requests
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and illegal activities
  • Analysis: Understand how users use our applications to improve performance and experience
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Children's Privacy

Special Protection for Children:

We take the protection of children's privacy under 13 years old very seriously. Our applications that are targeted at children or that may knowingly be used by children strictly follow the following guidelines:

4.1 Minimal Data Collection

In applications intended for children under 13 years old:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information without verifiable parental consent
  • We collect only information strictly necessary for the application to function
  • We do not use behavioral advertising or tracking
  • We do not share children's data with third parties, except essential service providers

4.2 Parental Consent

When necessary to collect information from children:

  • We request verifiable consent from parents or legal guardians
  • We provide parents with the ability to review and delete their children's data
  • We allow parents to refuse additional collection or use of their children's data

4.3 COPPA and Regulation Compliance

Our children's applications comply with:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) - United States
  • LGPD (General Data Protection Law) - Brazil, specific articles on children's data
  • GDPR-K (Age Appropriate Design Code) - European Union
  • Google Play Families Policy - Specific policies of the Google Play Store

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

5.1 Service Providers

We share data with trusted service providers who help us operate our applications:

  • Hosting and Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform, Amazon Web Services
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics
  • Crash Reporting Services: Firebase Crashlytics
  • Payment Systems: Google Play Billing, Apple In-App Purchase

All providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the specified purposes.

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal processes.

5.3 Protection of Rights

We may share information to protect the rights, property or safety of the company, our users or the public.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data transmitted is protected using TLS/SSL
  • Access Control: Restricted access to personal data only for authorized personnel
  • Monitoring: Intrusion detection systems and continuous security monitoring
  • Audits: Regular security and compliance reviews
  • Data Minimization: We collect only data strictly necessary

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

7.1 Access and Portability

You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you in a structured and readable format.

7.2 Correction

You can request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

7.3 Deletion

You can request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.

7.4 Processing Restriction

You can request that we limit the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

7.5 Objection

You can object to the processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests.

7.6 Consent Withdrawal

When processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise your rights: Contact us via email at privacy@alemsolucoes.com

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our web applications may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep your preferences and session active
  • Analyze application usage
  • Provide personalized content

You can control the use of cookies through your browser or device settings.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the services you requested
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

When your data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

10. International Data Transfer

Your data may be transferred and processed in countries outside your country of residence. We ensure that all international data transfers are protected by adequate safeguards, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy certifications (when applicable)
  • Contractual guarantees with service providers

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through:

  • In-app notification
  • Email (if provided)
  • Update of the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

We recommend that you review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Commitment to Transparency

This Privacy Policy was drafted to meet the highest standards of transparency and complies with international data protection regulations, including LGPD, GDPR, and COPPA.
