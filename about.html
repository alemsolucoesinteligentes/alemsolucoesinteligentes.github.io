About Alem

We are a data-oriented technology company, specialized in solving complex problems through mathematical modeling, data science and artificial intelligence.

What drives us

Our mission is to transform technical and scientific challenges into applicable technological solutions. We combine methodological rigor, quantitative knowledge and modern software engineering practices to create products and systems that solve real problems.

We operate in various sectors, including industry, finance, energy, agribusiness and innovation, always seeking to generate measurable impact for our clients.

Areas of expertise

  • Data Science, Machine Learning and Deep Learning
  • Mathematical modeling, optimization and numerical simulations
  • Synthetic data generation, anonymization and data quality
  • Data Engineering, scalable pipelines and MLOps
  • Development of solutions with LLMs and decision support systems

How we work

We adopt an end-to-end approach: we understand the business problem, define hypotheses and metrics, prototype solutions and produce robust and scalable deliverables.

We prioritize reproducibility, observability and knowledge transfer to client teams, ensuring that solutions are sustainable in the long term.

Technologies and practices

We use modern technologies such as Python, Spark, Databricks, relational and graph databases, in addition to consolidated MLOps practices, model versioning and CI/CD.

Our team combines solid technical training with practical experience in industrial and high-complexity quantitative projects.
